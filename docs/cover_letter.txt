Dear Editor-in-Chief,

I am pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "Multi-Label Chest Radiograph Pathology Detection and Localization via Focal-Loss DenseNet-121 and Grad-CAM Explainability" for consideration for publication as a Original Research Article in your journal.

Chest radiograph (CXR) interpretation is an essential diagnostic tool, but manual analysis is prone to delays and diagnostic variance. In this paper, we address key challenges in automated CXR analysis, specifically multi-label co-occurrence of pathologies, extreme class imbalance, and the black-box nature of deep neural networks.

Key contributions of our work include:
1. Dual-Task Architecture: Integration of 14-class multi-label classification on MIMIC-CXR and 15-category spatial pathology localization on VinBigData.
2. Focal Loss Re-weighting: Implementation of Binary Focal Loss (alpha=0.25, gamma=2.0) to dynamically down-weight easy background instances, achieving a +4.2% F1-score improvement over standard Binary Cross-Entropy loss.
3. Visual Interpretability: Integration of Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) into the final feature extraction layer of DenseNet-121 to produce clinically actionable saliency heatmaps.

We confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not currently under consideration for publication elsewhere. All authors have read and approved the submitted version of the manuscript.
